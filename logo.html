Keep the relic intact

Rules of the mark

✦

Honor the lockup

  • Use the provided PNG. Keep the rose and the arch together.
  • Give it breathing room — at least the height of the rose’s bloom on every side.
  • Prefer 150px tall in a compact nav; 300px (or the source file) for signs and mastheads.
  • On ink or leather, seat the mark on a small parchment plate so the white field reads as a shop sign, not a hole.
  • Write the full name in copy as Rosebrook RPG Goods.

Do not

  • Recolor the rose, the stem, or the gold edge to “match a theme.”
  • Stretch, outline, or drop a cheap bevel on the letters.
  • Set the mark smaller than ~72px wide — the arch collapses.
  • Place it on busy art without a parchment plate or clear field.
  • Rebuild the wordmark in a neighboring serif “close enough.”
